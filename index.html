Patch Notes
Latest updates and features in EmitHub.
New Features June 2026
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Control the app without touching your mouse. Press Ctrl+V to instantly paste and send files, and Ctrl+D to immediately destroy your workspace.
  • Chunked Resume: Large file transfers survive network drops. If a transfer is interrupted halfway, it automatically resumes right where it stopped instead of starting over.
  • Transfer Speed Graph: Keep track of your transfer performance with a real-time speed graph showing MB/s during uploads and downloads.
  • Auto-Destroy on Inactivity: Automatically self-destructs the vault after a set period of inactivity. Room owners can enable, disable, or customize the timeout duration during room creation.
  • Stealth Mode: Strip metadata like camera types, locations from photos, and author info from documents before sending. Choose to enable it via a simple popup when creating hosted or P2P links.
  • Color Themes: Personalize your interface with custom color themes tailored to your style, going beyond simple light and dark modes.
  • Compact Mode: A minimal and dense layout designed for power users who want to maximize screen space and view more content at once.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Use every feature and navigate the entire application interface completely hands-free using just your keyboard.
  • Transfer Nicknames: Give your file transfers custom names (like "Project Files") so they appear clearly in your activity panel instead of generic, confusing codes.
